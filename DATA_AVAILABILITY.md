# Data availability

This repository does not distribute individual-level survey data.

The source data include health outcomes, exact age, sex, sport discipline, exposure measures, record identifiers, expert-validation responses, and detailed recruitment information. Participant consent and ethics safeguards do not authorize unrestricted public release. These inputs and participant-level intermediate files remain in ignored local directories and are not part of the public Git history.

Public materials are limited to analysis code, manuscript sources, population-frame counts, and disclosure-reviewed aggregate results. The committed outputs are sufficient to inspect the reported estimates and diagnostics but not to reconstruct participant records.

Any request for access to individual-level data must be assessed separately by the study investigators and the relevant ethics and data-governance requirements. Public availability of this code does not imply that access will be granted.
